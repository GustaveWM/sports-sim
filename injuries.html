How the simulations use this list

NFL. A player ESPN lists as Out, Doubtful or on injured reserve is left out of that week's simulation and his carries and targets go to his teammates; Questionable players stay in. The team ratings move a little for missing defenders (about half the size the numbers suggest, because the rest did not hold up when tested on a second season); a missing offensive player is handled by the simulation, not by the ratings. The picks and parlays read the list when they are made.

NBA. A player ESPN lists as Out is left out of the simulation; Day-to-day plays in about 60% of the simulated games (Questionable 55%, Doubtful 10%, Probable 90%), and his minutes go to whoever plays. The team ratings do not change for who is missing, so a game line with a star out can be off: DraftKings' price knows, and the NBA picks use DraftKings' chance and only ask the model to agree.

Only as fresh as the last update (7, 8, noon, 4, 5 and 8 o'clock, Sunday to Friday): a player ruled out after it is not in. Return dates are ESPN's estimates and move; ROS is ESPN's own flag, a return after the last regular-season game, or a note saying so.

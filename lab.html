A standing experiment, not a tip sheet. Every slate the machine writes down a few parlays to each recipe below, before the games start, and grades them afterwards. Nothing is ever edited once written, so the record is honest out of sample. Said is the chance the model gave; hit is how often they landed. A recipe is honest when those two agree, and only worth money when return is positive.
